Deepfake PulseCheck

Multimodal deepfake analysis web app

A public web app where anyone can upload a short face video and get an experimental deepfake analysis. Instead of trusting a single signal, it combines five independent lines of evidence, from the faint pulse in a person's skin to whether their lips match their voice, and fuses them into one verdict. When the evidence is weak it says so: the result is evidence, not proof.

The backend has just started collecting data; accuracy results will be published once models are evaluated.
