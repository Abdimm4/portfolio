PulseCheck

Deepfake detection with pulse signals

A tool that aims to identify deepfake videos by checking for a real pulse. A live face shows tiny skin-color changes with every heartbeat (remote photoplethysmography, rPPG), which manipulated faces may not reproduce consistently. PulseCheck measures that pulse in several face regions and checks whether they agree, and it says "inconclusive" instead of guessing when the signal is too weak.
